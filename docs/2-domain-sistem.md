# BAB II - DOMAIN SISTEM

## 2.1 Latar Belakang Domain

### 2.1.1 Industri Alas Kaki Indonesia

Indonesia memiliki sejarah panjang dalam industri alas kaki, dengan ratusan brand lokal yang berkembang sejak era 1990-an. Industri ini mengalami transformasi signifikan dari manufaktur tradisional menjadi brand modern yang menggabungkan craftsmanship lokal dengan desain kontemporer.

**Karakteristik Industri:**
- **Heritage Craftsmanship** - Keahlian tradisional pembuatan sepatu secara manual
- **Local Materials** - Penggunaan bahan berkualitas dari dalam negeri
- **Design Evolution** - Perpaduan desain tradisional dan modern
- **Target Market** - Konsumen Indonesia yang menghargai produk lokal berkualitas
- **Distribution** - Mix antara retail stores fisik dan e-commerce

### 2.1.2 Tren Digital Transformation

Industri retail, termasuk fashion dan footwear, mengalami **digital transformation** yang pesat:

1. **E-Commerce Growth** - Peningkatan pembelian online
2. **Social Media Marketing** - Instagram dan TikTok sebagai platform utama
3. **Content Marketing** - Storytelling dan brand narrative
4. **Customer Engagement** - Direct interaction dengan konsumen
5. **Omnichannel Experience** - Integrasi online-offline

**Kebutuhan Digital:**
- ✅ Company website yang modern dan responsif
- ✅ Content Management System untuk update konten
- ✅ Social media integration
- ✅ Store locator dan informasi produk
- ✅ Customer testimonials dan engagement

## 2.3 Permasalahan di Domain Ini

### 2.3.1 Challenges dalam Digital Presence

#### **1. Content Management Challenges**

❌ **Problem:**
- Update konten memerlukan developer/technical person
- Tidak ada sistem untuk manage hero images, collections, testimonials
- Sulit untuk maintain consistency konten
- Tidak ada versioning atau history tracking

**Impact:**
- Slow response untuk marketing campaigns
- High maintenance cost
- Dependency pada technical team
- Missed opportunities untuk timely updates

#### **2. Product Information Management**

❌ **Problem:**
- Informasi koleksi produk tersebar (Instagram, website, stores)
- Tidak ada central database untuk product collections
- Sulit update informasi produk secara konsisten
- No image management system

**Impact:**
- Inconsistent product information
- Customer confusion
- Difficulty dalam showcasing new collections
- Poor user experience

#### **3. Store Locator Limitations**

❌ **Problem:**
- Store information sulit diupdate
- Tidak ada Google Maps integration
- Customer kesulitan menemukan toko terdekat
- Informasi jam operasional tidak real-time

**Impact:**
- Lost foot traffic
- Customer frustration
- Missed sales opportunities
- Poor customer service

#### **4. Customer Testimonials Management**

❌ **Problem:**
- Testimonials hanya di social media
- Tidak ada system untuk curate dan display testimonials
- Sulit showcase customer satisfaction
- No verification system

**Impact:**
- Low social proof pada website
- Reduced conversion rate
- Missed marketing opportunity
- Lack of trust building

#### **5. Contact & Lead Management**

❌ **Problem:**
- Contact form messages masuk ke email
- Tidak ada centralized inbox
- Sulit tracking dan follow-up
- No analytics pada customer inquiries

**Impact:**
- Slow response time
- Lost leads
- Poor customer service
- No data untuk improvement

### 2.3.2 Technical Challenges

#### **1. Non-Technical User Access**

❌ **Problem:**
- Marketing team tidak bisa update konten sendiri
- Memerlukan developer untuk setiap perubahan
- No user-friendly interface untuk content management

**Impact:**
- High operational cost
- Slow time-to-market
- Reduced agility
- Team frustration

#### **2. Content Versioning**

❌ **Problem:**
- Tidak ada history tracking untuk changes
- Tidak bisa rollback changes
- No audit trail

**Impact:**
- Risk of data loss
- Accountability issues
- Difficulty troubleshooting

#### **3. Image Management**

❌ **Problem:**
- Images disimpan randomly di folders
- No consistent naming convention
- Tidak ada image optimization
- No CDN integration

**Impact:**
- Slow page load
- Storage inefficiency
- Poor SEO
- Bad user experience

#### **4. Mobile Experience**

❌ **Problem:**
- Tidak semua page responsive
- Touch interactions tidak optimal
- Slow loading di mobile networks

**Impact:**
- High bounce rate di mobile
- Lost mobile traffic
- Poor mobile SEO
- Reduced conversions

### 2.3.3 Business Challenges

#### **1. Scalability Issues**

❌ **Problem:**
- Manual processes tidak scalable
- Sulit expand ke multiple stores
- Content updates bottleneck
- No automation

**Impact:**
- Limited growth potential
- High operational overhead
- Reduced competitiveness

#### **2. Data-Driven Decision Making**

❌ **Problem:**
- Tidak ada analytics untuk content performance
- No insights tentang user behavior
- Sulit measure ROI dari content
- No A/B testing capability

**Impact:**
- Decisions based on gut feeling
- Missed optimization opportunities
- Inefficient marketing spend

#### **3. Brand Consistency**

❌ **Problem:**
- Konten di berbagai channel tidak konsisten
- No centralized brand asset management
- Difficulty maintaining brand voice

**Impact:**
- Confused brand identity
- Reduced brand trust
- Poor brand recall

## 2.4 Solusi yang Diajukan

### 2.4.1 Comprehensive CMS Solution

#### **✅ Solution: Full-Featured Content Management System**

**Key Features:**

1. **User-Friendly CMS Interface**
   - Dashboard untuk overview
   - CRUD operations untuk semua content types
   - Role-based access control
   - Intuitive UI untuk non-technical users

2. **Hero Sections Management**
   - Upload dan manage hero carousel images
   - Edit titles, descriptions, CTAs
   - Display order management
   - Active/inactive toggle

3. **Collections Management**
   - Create dan edit product collections
   - Upload multiple images per collection
   - Rich text descriptions
   - SEO-friendly URLs
   - Display order control

4. **Store Locator System**
   - Add/edit/delete store locations
   - Google Maps integration
   - Store hours management
   - Contact information
   - Store images

5. **Testimonials Management**
   - Customer testimonial CRUD
   - Upload customer images
   - Rating system
   - Active/inactive control
   - Display order management

6. **Contact Messages Inbox**
   - Centralized message inbox
   - Mark as read/unread
   - Reply functionality
   - Search and filter
   - Export to CSV

7. **Company Information Management**
   - Edit About Us content
   - Manage company values
   - Founder profiles management
   - Social media links
   - SEO settings
